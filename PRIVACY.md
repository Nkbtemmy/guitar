# Guitaro privacy policy

*Last updated: 26 October 2026*

**Listening.** Guitaro uses your microphone only while something on screen is listening: a
lesson, the tuner, "check my chord" or the chord finder. On Android, "Find chords from a video"
can listen to the sound of the video playing inside Guitaro instead, after the system asks you.
Audio is analysed on your device as it arrives and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

**Your progress and songs** (lessons completed, stars, XP, streak, settings, favourites,
setlists, and the songs you write or find) are stored only on your device. Guitaro has no
accounts.

**Usage stats and crash reports.** To learn how many people use Guitaro and what to improve, the
app sends anonymous usage stats and crash reports through Google Firebase (Google Analytics for
Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics):

- which tab you open, which lessons you start and finish (with their stars), which of the
  bundled songs you open, and how many chords the chord finder found;
- the app version, device model, system version, language and country;
- for a crash, what the app was doing when it failed.

They're tied to a random ID that Firebase makes for this installation of the app, not to you.
They never include audio or what you play, your own songs (their titles or words), video links,
your name or email, or an advertising ID. Guitaro has no ads and doesn't track you across other
apps or websites.

You can turn this off at any time in **Me › Share anonymous usage stats**. Google processes this
data for Guitaro under the [Firebase terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
Analytics events are kept for 2 months, crash reports for 90 days.

**YouTube.** "Find chords from a video" plays the video in YouTube's own player inside the app,
so watching it works like watching on YouTube: Google receives the request and handles it under
the [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Lesson videos** are bundled with the app. If a build streams them from a server instead, the
device downloads each video file the same way a browser does, and nothing about you is sent
with the request.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Nkbtemmy/guitar.
