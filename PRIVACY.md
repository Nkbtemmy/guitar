# Fretwise privacy policy

Fretwise uses your microphone only while a lesson, the tuner, or "check my chord" is on screen,
to hear the notes and chords you play. Audio is analysed on your device as it arrives and is
never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Your progress (lessons completed, stars, XP, streak and settings) is stored only on your device.
Fretwise has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party tracking.

Lesson videos are bundled with the app. If a build streams them from a server instead, the
device downloads each video file the same way a browser does, and nothing about you is sent
with the request.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Nkbtemmy/guitar.
